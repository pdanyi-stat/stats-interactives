Exam prep

Practice for the final exam

A research methods final exam can be tougher than usual exams. Now you have access to (virtually) unlimited practice. Here you can generate unique exams related to two datasets. The exam will grade you, and then you can generate a new unique dataset to practice with again! You can turn on and off help with SPSS and variable codes, or try it under exam conditions with no help. All questions and grade keys shift along with your unique practice exam(s). You'll need SPSS to work through the exam, just like the real thing.
