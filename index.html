Data sources for all modules

County presidential returns compiled by Tony McGovern (tonmcg/US_County_Level_Election_Results_08-24); FiveThirtyEight's 2016 poll and state data; the British Election Panel Study and the 1988 Chilean plebiscite survey (carData); Florida 2000 county returns (carData); OpenIntro's midterm elections data; the 2004 American National Election Study; the Titanic passenger list (titanic3, Vanderbilt Biostatistics); the 2020 and 2024 American National Election Studies and the 2016 ANES Pilot Study; Pew Research Center's 2014 post-election survey; Gallup's January 2025 party-ideology results (respondent-level data imputed to match the published percentages); the 1995 South Africa election survey; the 1854 Massachusetts lunatics data; and a cross-national dataset of countries (World.sav, from the course files).
