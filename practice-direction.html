● Practice · Putting it all together

Which way does it run?

A sign on its own doesn't tell you what happened. You have to walk it through how the variables are coded. Each card gives you the coding, one piece of SPSS output and a claim. Say which way the relationship runs, in plain words, and whether the output supports the claim.

How to walk it through. Move the IV from its low code to its high code. The sign (or the change in the group means) tells you whether the DV moves toward its higher or lower codes. Then check what the higher codes mean for that DV. Last, check significance or strength, because a sign alone isn't enough to support a claim.
